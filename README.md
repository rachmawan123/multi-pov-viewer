# [**Multi POV Viewer**](https://rachmawan123.github.io/multi-pov-viewer/)

A lightweight multi-screen YouTube viewer designed for watching multiple player perspectives simultaneously.

**Watch multiple POVs at once. Listen to the player you want.**

## Features

- **Multi YouTube Playback** – Watch 4 or more YouTube videos simultaneously.
- **Dynamic Grid Layout** – Arrange videos into 1, 2, 3, or 4 columns.
- **Individual Fullscreen** – Expand any POV independently.
- **Individual Playback Control** – Play or pause each video separately.
- **Global Playback Control** – Play or pause all videos at once.
- **Spacebar Synchronization** – Control selected videos using the Space key.
- **Audio Solo Mode** – Listen to one player's audio while muting all other POVs.
- **Individual Mute** – Control audio for each player independently.
- **Sync Position** – Align playback positions across videos.
- **Custom POV Names** – Rename each player to make identification easier.

## How to Use

1. Open the Live Demo.
2. Copy YouTube video links.
3. Paste multiple links into the input box, one URL per line.
4. Click **ADD VIDEOS**.
5. Select your preferred grid layout.
6. Use the SOLO button to focus on a specific player's audio.
7. Press SPACE to play or pause videos with Follow Space enabled.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| SPACE | Play / Pause synchronized videos |
| F11 | Browser Fullscreen |
| ESC | Exit Fullscreen |

## Audio Controls

Use SOLO to unmute one selected POV and automatically mute all other POVs.

This is especially useful for multiplayer games with proximity voice chat, such as:

- Phasmophobia
- Among Us
- Mahjong multiplayer
- Other multiplayer games with voice communication

## Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
- Internet connection.
- YouTube videos must allow embedded playback.

## Important Notes

- This application uses the YouTube IFrame Player API.
- Some videos may not support embedded playback due to restrictions imposed by their owners.
- Playback synchronization is approximate and may vary depending on network conditions.
- YouTube content and playback remain subject to YouTube's terms and policies.
