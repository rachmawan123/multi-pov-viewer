PLAYBACK & SYNC
Offsets are saved in this browser
How offsets work: Pause streams at the same gameplay moment, then choose “Capture current alignment”. The app records each POV's time difference from the Master. A positive offset seeks further into that POV; a negative offset seeks earlier. Use “Apply offsets” whenever you want to realign.
Prepare & Start waits briefly for players to buffer, seeks each POV to its offset-adjusted position, then sends the play commands together. YouTube does not expose exact frame timing or a guaranteed buffer-ready signal, so perfect simultaneous starts cannot be guaranteed.
